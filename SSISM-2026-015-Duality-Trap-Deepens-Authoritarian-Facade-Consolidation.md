- Purpose: Gain external acceptance and reduce isolation

**Institutional / Iron Line**  
- Maintains doctrinal primacy (“Military as Parents”), command cohesion, and coercive operations  
- Continues daily kinetic activity (airstrikes, artillery, ground operations, arrests)

These two lines are **structurally interdependent yet in permanent tension**. The Mask Line requires softening language to gain diplomatic space. The Iron Line responds with escalated violence to reassure the officer corps that the center has not gone soft (**Compensation Effect**).

This is not “power migration” from military to civilian. It is **re-branding of the same apex authority** under increasing strain. The rolling machine of dual lines continues to crush people daily — through violence in the periphery and repression in the center.

The 2026 arrangement has not resolved the fundamental schism created by the coup. It has only made it more sophisticated and diplomatically presentable.
